Frequently Asked Questions

Need to know

Common questions

Where is Keyworld Music Institute located?

Training is available at FUTA, Akure, and Idi-Iroko, Oke Aro, Akure, as well as online.

What instruments do you teach?

We currently offer Piano/Keyboard, Guitar, Violin, Saxophone, Trumpet, Drum Set, Talking Drum and Voice Training.

Do you offer online music training?

Yes. Online training is available for learners who prefer remote study.

Who can enroll?

Our programmes support beginners, intermediate learners and advanced musicians, with placement depending on the learner and programme.

How much do the level programmes cost?

Level 1 is ₦120,000 for 3 months; Level 2 is ₦250,000 for 4 months; Level 3 is ₦400,000 for 3 months.

How do I enroll?

Use the online enrollment form on the Admissions page or contact us through WhatsApp or email.

Still have a question?

Contact Keyworld Music Institute directly for enrollment and programme information.

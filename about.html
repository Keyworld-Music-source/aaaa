About Keyworld Music Institute

A practical music education and performance training institute serving learners in Akure, Ondo State and online

Our approach

Structured learning with practical musicianship

Keyworld Music Institute is focused on helping learners build useful musical skills through a combination of music theory, aural training, ensemble work, instrument or voice performance and broader musicianship.

Our training supports beginners, intermediate learners and advanced musicians, with learning available at our Akure locations and online.
